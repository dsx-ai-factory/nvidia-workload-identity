# Code of Conduct

## The standard

Contributors to NVIDIA Workload Identity are expected to treat each other
professionally. Harassment, personal attacks, and discrimination are not
tolerated, in this repository or in any venue where it is discussed.

## Reporting

Raise a concern in [GitHub_Conduct@nvidia.com](mailto:GitHub_Conduct@nvidia.com). Reports are handled by the maintainers of
dsx-ai-factory and are kept confidential.

If the concern involves a maintainer, use [GitHub_Conduct@nvidia.com](mailto:GitHub_Conduct@nvidia.com) all the same:
maintainers recuse themselves from reports that name them. NVIDIA employees
have an additional route, through their management chain or Human Resources.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, commits, issues, and pull requests that breach
this standard, and may block accounts from the repository.
