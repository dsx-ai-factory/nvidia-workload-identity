# Governance

## Who decides

dsx-ai-factory/nvidia-workload-identity is maintained by the team listed in
CODEOWNERS. A change needs review from a code owner for the paths it touches.

## Becoming a maintainer

Maintainers are added by the existing maintainers after a sustained record of
review-quality contributions. Open an issue to start the conversation.

## Making a decision

Technical decisions are made in pull requests and issues, in the open. Where
maintainers disagree, the decision goes to the team listed in CODEOWNERS
for the affected path.

## Changing this document

Through a pull request against this file, reviewed by the maintainers.
